About the company

Karachi runs on four power sources. Nobody tells businesses which one to use.

GridWise is a student venture in energy analytics. We build a data-science platform that turns meter, inverter and generator data into clear, hour-by-hour energy decisions for commercial and industrial sites.

High electricity costs

Unreliable supply

Juggling four sources

Decisions made manually

Products & services

One dashboard. Four decisions it makes easier.

GridWise is a proposed subscription-based energy optimization dashboard. These are its four core MVP features.

Demand forecasting

Predicts each site's electricity load for the next 24 hours, hour by hour, using history, calendar and weather.

→ next-day peak & hourly load

Wastage alerts

Detects abnormal usage, like equipment left running after closing, by comparing actual load against the forecast.

→ ranked alerts with cost impact

Energy-mix planner

An optimization model picks the cheapest combination of grid, solar, battery and generator for every hour.

→ hour-by-hour action plan

Cost & savings

Compares the GridWise plan against current practice and reports cost, generator use and savings in rupees.

→ monthly report & export

Proposed project

From raw meter readings to an hour-by-hour energy plan.

A working prototype built on a big-data pipeline: streaming ingestion, Spark processing, machine-learning models and an optimizer, served to a role-based web app.

01 · Problem

Costly, blind energy decisions

Businesses in Karachi juggle grid, solar, batteries and generators during peak tariffs and outages. Existing tools (bills, inverter apps, manual logs) report past usage but never recommend the cheapest option.

02 · Solution

Predict, detect, optimize

  • Forecast next-day demand per site (Spark MLlib gradient-boosted trees)
  • Detect wastage from forecast residuals
  • Plan the cheapest source mix with linear programming
  • Show it all in one role-based dashboard
03 · Benefits

Clear actions, lower costs

  • Know tomorrow's peak before it happens
  • Catch equipment left running
  • Use less diesel during outages
  • See savings in rupees, not just kWh
