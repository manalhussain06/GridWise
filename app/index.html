Data platform pipeline

How a meter reading becomes a decision
All services healthy (demo)

1 · Sources

Smart meters, inverters, generator logs (MQTT/HTTP), CSV uploads, tariffs, weather API

2 · Ingest

Apache Kafka · topic telemetry.raw, 12 partitions by site_id

3 · Stream

Spark Structured Streaming · 10 s micro-batches, 10 min watermark, live alerts

4 · Lake

MinIO/S3 Parquet · bronze → silver → gold, quarantine zone

5 · Batch ML

Airflow DAG · Spark ETL, MLlib GBT forecast, anomaly scoring, LP planner · MLflow

6 · Serve

TimescaleDB + FastAPI (JWT, roles) → this app · Prometheus/Grafana
